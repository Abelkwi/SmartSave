What You Get

A real platform for real ideas

Not just a competition — an ongoing ecosystem for youth to build, test, and scale food systems solutions.

🏆
Hackathons & Challenges
Quarterly hackathons focused on real food system problems — storage, logistics, market access, nutrition — with funding for winning teams.
🔬
Research Grants
Funding and academic partnership support for university students and researchers studying food loss, agritech, and circular economy models.
💡
Idea Incubation
Submit an early-stage idea and get structured feedback, validation support, and a path toward piloting it on the SMARTSAVE platform.
🚀
Startup Acceleration
Promising ventures get matched with mentors, potential investors, and a fast-track to integrate with SMARTSAVE's farmer and buyer network.
🎓
Mentorship Network
Access experienced mentors from agritech, logistics, public health, and entrepreneurship — paired based on your project's specific needs.
🌍
National Recognition
Showcase your work to government agencies, NGOs, and private sector partners actively looking for youth-led solutions to scale.
