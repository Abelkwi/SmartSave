Hotels, Restaurants & Schools
List surplus food that would otherwise be discarded. Set pick-up times, specify quantities and food type — the platform handles the rest.
1
Log your surplus food with quantity, type, and pick-up window
2
SMARTSAVE admin verifies the listing and notifies matched NGOs
3
An NGO confirms pick-up — you receive confirmation and a receipt
🤝
NGOs & Community Organisations
Browse verified food donations by district and food type. Claim what your beneficiaries need and coordinate collection quickly.
1
Browse the live donation feed — filter by district, food type, quantity
2
Submit a claim request with your organisation details and need
3
Admin approves — collect food at the agreed time and confirm receipt
✅
SMARTSAVE Admin
Every donation and claim is reviewed by the platform team — ensuring food safety, fair distribution, and accountability at every step.
1
Review and verify each donation listing for food safety compliance
2
Match donations to NGOs based on proximity, capacity, and need
3
Track completion, generate impact reports, and flag any issues
