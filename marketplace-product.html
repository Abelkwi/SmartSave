About these tomatoes

These are fresh Roma tomatoes grown organically in Musanze District, Northern Province. Harvested at peak ripeness, they are firm-fleshed, richly flavoured, and ideal for sauces, salads, and cooked dishes. The cooperative uses traditional sun-drying methods alongside natural pest control — no synthetic pesticides are used at any stage of production.

These tomatoes are popular with hotels and restaurants across Kigali for their consistent size and deep colour. They are typically harvested Monday and Wednesday mornings and can be delivered same-day or next-day to Kigali and surrounding districts.

Available in bulk (50 kg+) or standard orders (minimum 10 kg). Custom packaging in 5 kg or 10 kg mesh bags is available upon request.

Delivery & Logistics

Coverage: Musanze, Kigali (Gasabo, Kicukiro, Nyarugenge), Rwamagana, and Huye. Delivery available for orders of 50 kg or more. Smaller orders can be collected from the cooperative's aggregation point in Musanze.

Lead time: Place orders before 6:00 PM for next-day delivery to Kigali. Same-day collection available from Musanze on harvest days (Monday & Wednesday).

Minimum for delivery: 50 kg. Orders under 50 kg are available for self-collection only.

Delivery cost: Negotiated directly with the cooperative based on volume and frequency. Recurring weekly delivery agreements are available at discounted logistics rates.

Payment: MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or invoice (net 15 days for verified institutional buyers).
