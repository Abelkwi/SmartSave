Why We Started

Food should never be the problem. It should be the solution.

Rwanda has made remarkable progress in agricultural transformation — yet thousands of smallholder farmers still lose a third of their harvest after picking it, while hotels and schools struggle to find reliable local suppliers, and vulnerable households go without enough food. We saw these challenges not as separate problems, but as one disconnected system. SMARTSAVE exists to reconnect it — linking farmers, buyers, NGOs, and youth innovators into a single ecosystem where every harvest creates value instead of loss.

Our Vision
To build a resilient, connected, and inclusive food system where every harvest creates lasting value, every community has access to nutritious food, and every young innovator contributes to Rwanda's sustainable prosperity.
Our Mission
To transform Rwanda's food system by reducing food loss, strengthening market connectivity, improving nutrition, and empowering youth through innovation, technology, and data-driven solutions that create lasting value for every community.
Guiding Principles

What we stand on

Six principles shape every decision, partnership, and feature we build.

01
Save Before Producing More
Reducing post-harvest losses is often more sustainable and cost-effective than increasing production alone.
02
Connect Before Building
We prioritise connecting existing actors, institutions, and initiatives before creating parallel structures.
03
Empower, Don't Replace
SMARTSAVE complements and strengthens existing government programs, cooperatives, and private initiatives.
04
Data for Impact
Reliable data and evidence inform our planning, market coordination, monitoring, and continuous improvement.
05
Youth as Co-Creators
Young people are innovators, entrepreneurs, and community leaders — not merely beneficiaries of the platform.
06
Sustainability by Design
Every intervention is developed with long-term environmental, financial, and social sustainability in mind.
Our Journey

From idea to ecosystem

Early 2025
The Idea Takes Shape
A small team of youth innovators began researching food loss across Rwanda's value chain, identifying the disconnect between farmers, buyers, and surplus food.
Mid 2025
First Cooperative Partnerships
SMARTSAVE began piloting with farming cooperatives in Musanze and Huye districts, testing direct market connections with local hotels and schools.
Late 2025
Platform Concept Validated
With early results showing increased farmer income and reduced losses, the team began designing the full digital platform — marketplace, food recovery, and innovation hub.
2026
Building the Ecosystem
SMARTSAVE is now building out its full platform — connecting farmers, buyers, NGOs, and youth innovators nationwide, with plans to scale to all 30 districts.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SMARTSAVE free to use for farmers?

Yes. Registration and listing produce on SMARTSAVE is completely free for farmers and cooperatives. We never charge fees to join the platform.

How does SMARTSAVE verify donors and NGOs?

Every donor and NGO account is reviewed by our admin team before activation. We verify registration documents, contact details, and operating history to ensure safe, accountable food transfers.

What payment methods are supported?

We're building support for MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money for direct farmer payments, alongside invoicing for institutional buyers. This is being rolled out as we move from prototype to full platform.

Can youth innovators outside Rwanda apply?

The Innovation Hub currently focuses on Rwandan students and youth innovators, but we welcome partnerships and collaboration proposals from innovators and institutions across East Africa.

How can my organisation become a partner?

Reach out through the contact form above with a short description of your organisation and proposed collaboration. Our partnerships team will follow up within a few business days.
