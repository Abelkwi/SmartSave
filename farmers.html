How It Works

From registration to your first sale

Four simple steps to start reaching buyers, listing produce, and growing your farm income.

1
Register for Free
Sign up with your email address, district, and cooperative. Verification takes less than 24 hours.
2
List Your Produce
Add your available crops with quantities, harvest date, and price. Your listings are visible to hundreds of verified buyers.
3
Receive Orders
Buyers send purchase orders directly to you. Review, confirm, and arrange delivery on your schedule.
4
Get Paid
Payment is confirmed via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money directly to your account — no delays, no middlemen.

Why Farmers Choose SMARTSAVE

Tools built for Rwanda's real farmers

Live Market Prices
See today's prices for your crops across Kigali and all district markets — so you never sell below the going rate again.
Direct Buyer Connections
Receive purchase orders directly from verified hotels, schools, restaurants, and retailers — with agreed prices upfront.
Storage & Loss Reduction Tips
Crop-specific guidance on post-harvest handling, affordable storage solutions, and preservation techniques to protect your income.
Income Dashboard
Track every sale, order, and payment in one place. See seasonal trends and plan next season's planting with real data.
Cooperative & Group Tools
Cooperatives can manage members, aggregate supply, and fulfill larger institutional orders that individual farmers couldn't handle alone.
