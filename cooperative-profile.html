About the Cooperative

Akagera Farmers Cooperative was founded in 2018 with the mission of strengthening market access for smallholder farmers in Musanze District. Today, the cooperative brings together 280 member farmers across 6 sectors, collectively managing over 340 hectares of productive land in the fertile volcanic soils of the Northern Province.

The cooperative specialises in vegetables, legumes, and root crops — with organic certification covering all member farms. Through SMARTSAVE, Akagera has connected to institutional buyers in Kigali and across the country, enabling the cooperative to fulfill bulk orders that individual farmers could not handle alone.

Key programmes include shared storage facilities, group transport to Kigali markets twice weekly, and a training programme for post-harvest handling and soil health. The cooperative also supports a women farmers group representing 62% of membership.
