Before You Write

Frequently Asked Questions

You might find your answer here first.

How long does it take to receive a response?
For messages submitted through the contact form, we respond within 4 business hours Monday–Friday. WhatsApp enquiries are usually answered within 2 hours during office hours (8 AM–6 PM EAT). If you submit outside of office hours, you will receive a response first thing the next working morning.
I'm a farmer and I'm having trouble with my dashboard. Who should I contact?
Email farmers@smartsave.rw or WhatsApp us at +250 788 000 000. Include your registered email address and a brief description of the issue and our farmer support team will resolve it within a few hours. For registration questions, you can also visit the Farmers Portal page.
I represent an NGO. How do I access available food donations?
Start by registering your organisation at register-ngo.html. Once your account is verified (usually within 24–48 hours), you can access the live donation feed on the Food Recovery page. For urgent food requests, contact recovery@smartsave.rw directly.
I'm a journalist writing about food systems in Rwanda. How do I request an interview?
Email press@smartsave.rw with your publication name, the angle of your story, your preferred interview format, and your deadline. We respond to press enquiries within 24 hours and can usually accommodate interviews within 48–72 hours. We also have a press kit with key statistics, photography, and spokesperson bios available on request.
My organisation would like to partner with SMARTSAVE. What's the first step?
Send a brief introduction to partners@smartsave.rw — one or two paragraphs describing your organisation, what you work on, and what kind of collaboration you have in mind. We review all partnership enquiries and follow up within 48 hours to explore alignment and next steps.
Is SMARTSAVE available outside Rwanda?
SMARTSAVE currently operates within Rwanda across all 30 districts. We welcome enquiries from organisations in other East African countries who are interested in the model, potential licensing, or research collaboration. If you're from outside Rwanda, select "Partner" in the contact form and describe your interest — our partnerships team will follow up.
