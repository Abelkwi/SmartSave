The Challenge

Food is being lost before it can nourish anyone

Despite Rwanda's remarkable agricultural progress, interconnected challenges continue to hold the food system back.

Post-Harvest Food Loss
Up to 30% of food is lost after harvest due to poor storage, handling, and lack of market connections — costing farmers income they've already earned.
Fragmented Market Access
Hotels, schools, and restaurants struggle to source reliable local produce while farmers lack access to institutional buyers — both sides miss out.
Nutrition & Hunger Gap
Over 1 in 4 children under five experience stunting — yet edible food is lost every day. The problem isn't production alone; it's access and distribution.
Untapped Youth Potential
Thousands of young graduates have the skills and ambition to transform Rwanda's food system — but lack pathways to apply them meaningfully.

Platform Features

Built for every role in the food system

Nine platform modules — purpose-built for farmers, buyers, institutions, NGOs, youth innovators, and administrators.

Farmer Dashboard
Manage listings, track orders, view earnings, monitor crop status, and receive personalized market alerts — all in one place.
Marketplace
Search, filter, and order verified local produce by district, price, availability, and certification — from vegetables to coffee and dairy.
Market Intelligence
Live price feeds, seasonal demand forecasts, and district-level supply analytics to help producers and buyers plan with confidence.
Food Recovery
Coordinate surplus food donations from institutions and direct them efficiently to NGOs and vulnerable households across Rwanda.
District Analytics
Interactive maps showing food loss hotspots, market demand by region, and coverage gaps — enabling targeted interventions across all 30 districts.
Partnership Hub
Connect government agencies, NGOs, universities, cooperatives, and private companies into coordinated initiatives that multiply impact.
Buyer Portal
Manage orders, track deliveries, schedule recurring supply agreements, and build direct relationships with verified cooperatives and farms.
Innovation Hub
Hackathons, startup incubation, research collaboration, and mentorship — connecting youth innovators with real food system challenges to solve.
Learning & Resources
Agricultural best practices, storage guides, market analysis reports, and training content — accessible to every member on the platform.
